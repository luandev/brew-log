# Pear & Maple Wine

## Summary

Small-batch pear and maple wine started on 2026-09-05 from fresh pear juice produced during the same juice-extraction session as batch 2026-005. The pear juice was notably sweet before adjustment. Approximately 100 mL of maple syrup was added, producing a hydrometer reading of 12% potential alcohol. The exact specific gravity was not recorded. The batch volume is exactly 1.7 L, with two cinnamon sticks in the PET fermentation vessel. Pectic enzyme was used during juice extraction. Yeast has been pitched and primary fermentation is active; yeast nutrient is planned for 2026-09-06.
