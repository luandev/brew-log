## Process

1. Sanitise the fermenter, tools, juicer, and anything contacting the must.
2. Extract the apple and pear juices, using pectic enzyme during both juice-extraction steps.
3. Combine approximately 3 L apple juice, 1.5 L pear juice, and 0.5 L cranberry juice.
4. Add 2 kg honey.
5. After the first hydrometer sample reads above the useful range, add water; the exact amount was not recorded.
6. Add two cinnamon sticks in a tea bag.
7. Pitch the high-alcohol-tolerance wine yeast and begin primary fermentation.
8. On 2026-09-06, add yeast nutrient according to the product instructions and record the product/dose.
9. Take a hydrometer reading on 2026-09-06. If gravity remains excessively high, dilute with water or cranberry juice and remeasure.
10. Continue primary fermentation and use later gravity readings to decide when the batch is ready for secondary.
