## Process

1. Begin with approximately 1 L fresh yard-apple juice.
2. Prepare approximately 1 L tea using black tea, mint, and Whittard Plum & Cinnamon instant tea and combine with the apple base.
3. Take the first potential-alcohol reading of 10%.
4. Increase fermentable sugar by an unrecorded amount; record the later reading of approximately 11% potential alcohol.
5. Ferment the approximately 2 L must in PET away from direct sunlight.
6. Continue primary fermentation until the batch is confirmed in active secondary by 2026-09-04.
7. On 2026-09-05, rack carefully into a clean PET vessel, add pectic enzyme according to product instructions, minimize splashing/headspace, and return to an airlock.
8. Allow fermentation and clarification to continue together while monitoring activity.
9. Take the scheduled hydrometer reading and repeat it after roughly three days.
10. When gravity is stable, begin the formal clearing period and let sediment settle under airlock.
11. Rack once more off the clarified sediment when a useful sediment layer has formed.
12. Rest briefly after the final rack and take a final stability reading.
13. Bottle as still wine with no priming sugar only after stability is confirmed.
14. Age in bottle and record tasting development.

## Quality Plan

Keep oxygen exposure low during racking, keep the PET away from direct sunlight, avoid bottling while gravity is still changing, and let the tea/herb/spice profile integrate during clearing and maturation. If the wine remains hazy after fermentation is fully stable, extend clearing rather than adding unrecorded finings by assumption.
