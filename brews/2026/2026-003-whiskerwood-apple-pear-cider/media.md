# Media

Hydrometer, fruit, fermenter and bottling/sanitation images were shared during the brew conversation; the cider label is the first image committed for this batch.

## Label / naming history

- Early label work used the working name **Whiskerwood**.
- The final brew name became **The Three Cats**.
- Later title work played visually on **Three / Tree**, while retaining "The Three Cats" as the actual name.
- Label concepts used a vintage orchard / engraved treatment suitable for thermal printing.
- Historical label copy referenced apple and pear, cinnamon, natural carbonation / bottle conditioning and vanilla-infused priming sugar.
- Any ABV printed on exploratory label drafts should not be treated as a measured `actual_abv`; the brew log does not contain a confirmed stable final-gravity pair sufficient to promote one of those draft values to a measured result.
