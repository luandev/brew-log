# Whiskerwood Apple & Pear Cider

## Summary

Small-batch apple and pear cider made from freshly juiced fruit, water, supermarket apple juice with added vitamin C, Turkish apple-flavoured sugar, cinnamon, and wine yeast. Fermented in a 10 L bucket with an airlock. The batch is near the end of primary fermentation and is awaiting a confirmation gravity reading before bottle conditioning.
