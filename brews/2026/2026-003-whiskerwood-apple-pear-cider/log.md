# Brew Log

## 2026-08-16 — Day 1

**Time:**  
**Stage:** Preparing / primary fermentation

### Measurements

- Plain water added: approximately 1–1.5 L.
- Total volume after supermarket apple juice was added: approximately 6–7 L.
- Cider temperature during hydrometer reading: about 20 °C.
- Room temperature during setup: about 24 °C.
- Starting gravity from hydrometer photo: approximately 1.038–1.040.

### Actions

- Fresh apples and pears were juiced.
- The fresh juice was pasteurised and allowed to cool.
- Approximately 45–50 g Turkish apple-flavoured sugar was added.
- Plain water was added during setup.
- Supermarket apple juice containing added vitamin C was added to increase the batch volume.
- A hydrometer reading was taken before fermentation.
- Dry wine yeast was pitched into a 10 L bucket and the airlock was fitted.
- Cinnamon sticks were present during primary fermentation; exact quantity was not recorded.

### Observation

The final setup used a 10 L fermenter for approximately 6–7 L of cider. The hydrometer's potential-alcohol scale was also described during setup as reading just under 7%; the specific scale value was not recorded as a gravity measurement.

### Next

- Monitor primary fermentation.
- Keep the fermenter airlocked and out of direct sunlight.

## 2026-09-01 — Day 17

**Time:**  
**Stage:** Primary fermentation — near completion

### Measurements

- On Day 3, airlock activity was described as very vigorous.
- Around the end of the first week, airlock activity had slowed to about one bubble every 40 seconds.
- A later hydrometer reading was 1.000.
- A second stable gravity reading has not yet been recorded.

### Actions

- The fermenter was shaken once daily during approximately the first three days, then left undisturbed.
- The fermenter was opened once for roughly five minutes to take a hydrometer reading.
- Beer bottles were collected for bottle conditioning.
- NoRinze sanitiser and a bottle rinser/sprayer were acquired for bottling preparation.
- The planned priming rate was updated to 5 g sugar per litre.

### Observation

The cider aroma was described as strong but not foul, then clarified as wine-like and fruity. Cinnamon sticks had been forgotten in the fermenter during primary; their removal has not been confirmed. No bottling has been reported yet.

### Next

- Take a confirmation hydrometer reading before bottling.
- Confirm the cinnamon sticks are removed before bottling.
- If gravity is stable, siphon gently into sanitised beer bottles, prime at 5 g/L, and crown-cap.
- Bottle-condition, then continue aging toward Christmas.
