## Process

1. Ferment the apple wine base with yeast and rapadura.
2. Record the hydrometer potential-alcohol reading of 11% taken during the batch.
3. Brew 1 packet Lidl Very Berry tea with 3 heaped spoonfuls of sugar, cool it, and combine it with the wine.
4. Continue fermentation in PET away from direct sunlight.
5. Rack off sediment into a clean PET vessel.
6. Dissolve 90 g pure maple syrup in 160 mL hot water and add it for secondary fermentation.
7. Keep under airlock in a dark storage area.
8. Take the 2026-09-04 tasting sample.
9. On 2026-09-05, rack again into a clean PET vessel, add pectic enzyme according to the product instructions, and return to an airlock.
10. Allow clarification to proceed while secondary fermentation finishes.
11. Take a hydrometer reading on the scheduled checkpoint and repeat it to confirm stability.
12. Once stable, rack off clarification sediment with minimal splashing.
13. If desired, trial a very small orange-zest addition and/or bench-test finished-wine sweetener; record any actual addition.
14. After any finishing adjustment, confirm that gravity remains stable and no renewed fermentation occurs.
15. Bottle as still wine with no priming sugar.
16. Age in bottle and record tasting changes over time.
